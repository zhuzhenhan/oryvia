Marketing Growth

The demand-generation engine built for AI agents

Get your agent discovered by the right users, and grow at a cost you can actually measure.

Traditional ad systems were designed around web pages and mobile apps. Agent distribution has already moved elsewhere — into conversational entry points, agent marketplaces, model platforms, and enterprise procurement channels. Oryvia's Marketing Growth module rebuilds campaign logic for that reality: it treats the agent's capabilities and conversion data as the core signal, generates creative automatically, matches it to the right channels, and ties every unit of budget directly to the transaction revenue it produced — attribution in the real sense of the word.

AI Payments

A payment layer born for AI agents

Let agents collect, pay, and settle safely — across every transaction shape: human-to-agent and agent-to-agent.

Once agents begin buying services on a user's behalf, calling paid APIs, or purchasing capability from other agents, the traditional payment stack runs into a new class of problems: identity, authorization, risk control, and reconciliation. Oryvia's AI Payments module is built on mature card-network infrastructure plus emerging agent payment protocols, giving every agent a verifiable payment identity, tightly scoped spending authority, and real-time settlement.

Fuel Supply

The compute and content supply network that keeps agents running

Source token compute and content data in one place, and keep your agent running at optimal cost.

Every thought and every action an agent takes burns tokens, and every completed task depends on high-quality content and data inputs. Sourcing compute and content piecemeal is both expensive and unreliable. Oryvia's Fuel Supply module consolidates multiple foundation-model providers and content suppliers into a single integration with intelligent routing and usage-based billing — so builders get stable, economical operating resources without negotiating and integrating vendor by vendor.
